# Code of conduct

People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome to use and
contribute to logfmt. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, including interactions with maintainers.

## Working together

- Be considerate and patient with people learning Go or the project.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about code and ideas.
- Explain disagreements with examples or evidence, and accept corrections.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, privacy, and time.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, personal attacks,
and publishing someone's private information without permission are not
acceptable. Neither is retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock conversations,
or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors. The response
should reflect the behavior and its impact, and leave room to correct mistakes.

For a project moderation request that is safe to discuss publicly, contact
[@JohanLindvall](https://github.com/JohanLindvall) in the relevant issue or pull
request. Do not post private details or sensitive reports in a public thread.

For abuse or harassment, use GitHub's **Report content** menu on the relevant
issue, pull request, or comment, or report the user from their profile.
[GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
explain the available routes, including contacting GitHub Support privately.
Reports sent to GitHub Support are handled by GitHub, not by this project's
maintainer. This route also applies to concerns about a maintainer.

For vulnerabilities in the library, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
